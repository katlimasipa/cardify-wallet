## Design Context

### Users
People in South Africa who want fast, reliable access to loyalty and membership cards at checkout, especially on mobile.

### Brand Personality
Assured, discreet, crafted.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained digital wallet with editorial typography, physical card proportions, precise hairlines, and subtle material depth. The official geometric mark and wordmark lead the identity. Avoid generic dashboard cards, decorative glows, and excessive iconography.

### Design Principles
- Make the card itself the primary object.
- Keep checkout actions reachable with one hand.
- Use space and typography for hierarchy instead of containers.
- Preserve clarity on the smallest supported screens.
- Motion should feel quick and deliberate, never decorative.
